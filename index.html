What I actually wanted to say

Rohan,

I don't know if making a whole website to tell you this is completely normal. Probably not.

But I wanted to do something that could hold everything I couldn't quite say properly.

I know things didn't work out the way we wanted them to. I know we weren't able to give each other enough time. And eventually, that became one of the things that hurt us.

But when I look back at everything, I don't only remember the difficult parts.

I remember us. The person I met at school. The person I've known for four years. The person I've spent almost two years with.

I don't want to pretend that everything will suddenly become perfect. It won't.

Life will still get busy. There will still be obstacles. We'll still have bad days.

But maybe if we ever get another chance, we can actually make space for each other.

Not every minute. Not some impossible promise. Just enough to remind each other that we're still important.

I'm not asking you to forget what happened.

I'm asking if you'd be willing to see whether what we had is worth trying for again.

Because after four years, I still don't think you're someone I want to call a chapter.
